## 1. Design a professional, memorable logo and full brand identity (color palette, typography, iconography, brand voice/style guide)

### Ideation

**The unifying concept.** An RV on the rim of the Grand Canyon. Open road, desert rock, copper sun on metal and dirt. That is Dealer Ultimate: not another blue SaaS dashboard in a vacuum, but dealership growth tools that belong next to the industry they serve (RV first, lot and landscape, not generic auto chrome). Every major brand choice traces back to that frame.

**Color.** Copper is the accent because of that ground: canyon wall, heat, trail dust, steel warmed by sun. It is also a deliberate break from the competitive set in the brief. Lime (Stick & Tin), sky cyan (Coast), electric blue (Fullpath / old DealerNerds blues), purple (DriveCentric), bright gold (Affinitiv) already own the category’s cool-tech and candy accents. We take the opposite lane: one warm metal, no second brand hue.

**Mark.** The logo pays homage to tyre tracks: a split chevron, thick and directional, with slightly eased corners (not razor squares, not soft bubbles). Same geometry as a tread biting into dirt, simplified into a monogram. Copper fill. It should feel machined and outdoor-American, not app-icon generic.

**Voice: “deal.”** The name carries the word *deal*. A deal is simple: I put something on the table, you put something on the table, we agree on the terms. Brand voice stays professional but conversational. No stiff enterprise throat-clearing, no empty “synergy” SaaS. Plain speech, clear exchange, then the ask.

**Copy cadence.** Body copy follows the deal shape, not a formal brochure:

> X does not have to be hard. I do Y and get you X. Deal?

That closer (“Deal?”) is the brand handshake: use it on section finishes, CTAs, and key proof beats so the page keeps offering a fair trade. Do not paste the same sentence on every paragraph; keep the *shape* (give → get → agree) and land “Deal?” where a human would actually stick out a hand.

**Layout & chrome (same logic: stand apart).** Comps lean on pill buttons and flat full-bleed slabs. We do the opposite within the same category: **8px** corners (slight, never pill), **inset** modules, **floating grids** with a gentle drop shadow. Type stays clean and modern (Gotham + Inter) so “outdoors / Southwest” lives in color, mark, and language, not in costume western fonts.

**One-line brief.** Dealer Ultimate is the RV-lot partner that talks like a handshake and looks like canyon copper: tyre-track mark, warm accent against cool-tech competitors, conversational “I do this / you get that / Deal?” voice, and a slightly rounded, floating, inset site that does not clone the pill-and-neon dealer SaaS crowd.

### Client mandate (from Source B — what they said to borrow / have / imitate)

**Vibe:** B2B dealership-tech; confident, modern, data-driven; demo requests from owners + marketing directors; tech-forward not stiff; bold type; punchy one-liners; speed/momentum.

**Borrow by source (client words):**

| Source | Borrow |
| --- | --- |
| Coast + Fullpath | Bold hero + short support; **large stat callouts**; icon-led feature grids |
| Affinitiv | **“I’m looking for a way to…” interactive menu**; OEM/partner logo strip; solutions by **sales / service / retention** |
| DriveCentric | Benefit-first messaging; **product UI / chat-bubble mockups** in page; quotes with **name + title + dealership** |
| Stick & Tin | **RV-native** vocab, imagery, positioning (not generic auto) |
| Old DealerNerds | **Conversation channels** (walk-in, call, chat, text, leads/email); proprietary **method framework** |

**Spine (client 1–8, all required):**  
(1) Hero + value prop + Get a Demo → (2) Trust bar → (3) Problem/solution (Affinitiv menu and/or 3-col grid) → (4) Features by area + visual → (5) Big stats → (6) Named testimonials → (7) Closing CTA → (8) Footer (services, resources, contact).

**Visual (client):** Bold sans headlines; clean body; dark hero + bright accent; whitespace; in-context product mocks not stock; icon callouts over long paragraphs.  
**Constraint (client):** Inspiration not clone; distinct logo, color, voice; RV-first.

Copper accent · 8px corners · inset floating grids · Deal? voice · outdoor/Southwest frame — not lime/cyan/blue/purple/gold or pill SaaS.
